ABOUT PITSTOP.CO

For the love of little cars.

PitStop.co is an independent shop for diecast car collectors. We bring together model cars from a range of brands, scales, and styles so you can find the next piece for your collection.

Every listing includes the details that matter when choosing a model, including its scale, condition, price, and availability. Our catalog changes as new models arrive.

A thoughtful way to collect

  • Browse models by name, brand, category, price, and availability.
  • Check each listing for model details and current stock.
  • Get in touch if you need more information before ordering.
